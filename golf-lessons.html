Why Take Golf Lessons?

Golf can be difficult when you're first starting out. There are a lot of things to think about, including your grip, stance, swing, and putting. Taking a lesson can help you understand the basics instead of trying to figure everything out by yourself.

For University of Iowa students who are interested in learning golf, lessons can also be a way to become more comfortable before playing a full round. You don't have to be an experienced golfer to benefit from instruction.

Types of Beginner Golf Lessons

One-on-One

Private Golf Lessons

Private lessons focus on one golfer at a time. An instructor can help with specific areas like driving, irons, chipping, or putting.

Best for: Golfers who want individual feedback.

Learn Together

Group Golf Lessons

Group lessons allow several people to learn the basics together. They can be a more relaxed option for beginners who want to try golf with friends.

Best for: Friends and first-time golfers.

First Steps

Beginner Golf Clinics

Beginner clinics usually focus on basic skills such as grip, stance, putting, and golf etiquette. They can be a good introduction before taking private lessons.

Best for: People who have never played golf.

What to Expect During Your First Golf Lesson

Your first golf lesson doesn't need to be stressful. Most beginners start by learning basic movements before worrying about hitting the ball far.

  • Start with a basic introduction to the equipment.
  • Learn how to hold a golf club correctly.
  • Practice your stance and setup.
  • Work on making controlled swings.
  • Try a few short shots or putting exercises.
  • Ask what you should practice before the next session.

How to Improve Between Lessons

Taking lessons is helpful, but practicing between sessions is important too. You don't need to spend hours at the driving range to see improvement.

  • Practice your grip and stance at home.
  • Spend time putting instead of only hitting drivers.
  • Focus on making consistent contact before swinging harder.
  • Practice for shorter periods rather than getting frustrated.
  • Keep track of one or two things you're trying to improve.

Frequently Asked Questions About Golf Lessons

Do I need my own golf clubs for lessons?

Not necessarily. Some instructors and practice facilities may offer equipment for beginners. It's a good idea to ask before attending.

How many golf lessons should a beginner take?

There's no required number. Some beginners start with one lesson to learn the basics, while others prefer several sessions to build consistency over time.

Are private or group lessons better for beginners?

Both can be helpful. Group lessons are good for learning with friends, while private lessons provide more individualized attention.

Can college students learn golf on a budget?

Yes. Group instruction and shorter practice sessions can help reduce costs. Students can also compare available discounts and practice options before spending money.
