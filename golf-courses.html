What Matters When Picking a Golf Course?

When comparing golf courses near Iowa City, it helps to consider more than just the course name or location.

  • Number of holes: Nine holes can fit a busy schedule better than eighteen.
  • Course difficulty: Beginners may prefer shorter courses with wider fairways.
  • Practice facilities: A putting green or driving range can help you warm up.
  • Walking options: Check whether walking is allowed or carts are required.
  • Tee times: Compare availability and booking policies.
  • Cost: Look into student rates and less expensive times to play.

Which Golf Course Is Right for You?

If you're just starting, a beginner-friendly nine-hole course might be the best choice. It gives you the opportunity to enjoy the game without committing to a long round.

Golfers looking for a bigger challenge may prefer a full eighteen-hole course. A short course can also be useful for people who mainly want to practice.
