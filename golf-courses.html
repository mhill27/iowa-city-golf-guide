Iowa City golf courses: find your fit

Three fictional examples show what to look for when choosing a course around Iowa City, from shorter layouts to a full championship-style round.

Three course styles to compare

Beginner-friendly

Prairie Bend Golf Club

An imaginary nine-hole layout with broad fairways, shorter holes, and a relaxed atmosphere. A good example of the kind of course that can make a first round less intimidating.

Best for: New golfers and quick afternoon rounds.

Full-length golf

Cedar Ridge Championship Course

This fictional eighteen-hole option features longer holes, more bunkers, and strategic tee shots. It illustrates the experience of a traditional full round.

Best for: Golfers who want a longer challenge.

Practice and short game

Hawkeye Links Short Course

An invented par-three-focused course with practice greens and shorter holes. It's a useful model for golfers who want to practice wedges, putting, and approach shots.

Best for: Practice sessions and casual group outings.

What matters when picking a course?

  • Number of holes: Nine holes may fit a busy day better than eighteen.
  • Course length: Shorter layouts can be more enjoyable for newer players.
  • Practice space: A driving range or putting green can help before a round.
  • Walking policies: Check whether walking is allowed or a cart is required.
  • Booking details: Compare tee-time availability and cancellation rules.
