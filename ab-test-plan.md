# A/B Test Plan — Iowa City Golf Guide

## Page and Variable

I will test the main headline on the Affordable Golf page.

- **Version A:** Affordable Golf in Iowa City
- **Version B:** Play More Golf in Iowa City Without Breaking the Bank

The headline is the only element that will change between versions.

## Hypothesis

I predict Version B will lead to a higher percentage of positive responses because it emphasizes the benefit of saving money while playing golf.

## Target Audience

An Iowa City college student who enjoys golf but wants to find affordable ways to play.

## AI Visitor Procedure

Each trial will use a fresh conversation with the same AI model and settings. The assigned page will be supplied as full page text, with all content identical except the headline.

**Exact prompt:**

"You are a college student in Iowa City who enjoys golf but has a limited budget. Read the following webpage as someone looking for affordable local golf options. Based only on the information presented, would you use this guide to help choose where to play golf? Answer YES or NO, followed by one sentence explaining your choice."

I will use the same prompt for every trial. The full assigned page text will follow the prompt in the same format.

**AI model and version:** OpenAI ChatGPT — GPT-6.
## Random Assignment

I will create a randomized list containing 50 assignments to Version A and 50 assignments to Version B before running the experiment. I will follow that list in order.

## Primary Outcome

The primary outcome is whether the AI visitor responds YES or NO to using the guide.

## Sample Size

100 AI visitor trials total:
- 50 trials for Version A
- 50 trials for Version B

I will complete all 100 trials regardless of interim results.

## Analysis

I will calculate the percentage of YES responses in each group and compare the two percentages using a two-proportion statistical test.

I will consider the hypothesis supported if Version B has a higher YES rate than Version A and the difference is statistically significant at the 0.05 level.

## Timeline

- Plan committed: Week of November 9, 2026, or earlier
- Experiment launched: By November 17, 2026
- Data collection completed: November 24, 2026
- Results included in final report: December 1, 2026

## Limitations

AI responses may not represent actual consumer behavior. The experiment measures simulated willingness to use the guide, not real website clicks, bookings, or purchases.
