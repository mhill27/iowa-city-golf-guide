How to Choose the Right Golf Experience in Iowa City

The best golf outing depends on your experience, schedule, and budget. If you're still learning, consider starting with nine holes instead of eighteen so you can practice without committing an entire afternoon. A shorter round can also make it easier to focus on enjoying the game rather than worrying about your score. Before choosing a course, think about whether you'd rather walk or ride, how comfortable you feel with longer holes, and whether you need access to a practice area.

University of Iowa students may also want to compare weekday and weekend playing options, transportation, and equipment needs. Student Golf Checklist: Before Your First Round

Planning your first golf outing in Iowa City? Here are a few things to consider before heading to the course.

  • Decide whether to play nine or eighteen holes.
  • Check whether you can borrow or rent golf clubs.
  • Compare walking rates and other available options.
  • Review basic golf etiquette before playing.
  • Plan your budget before booking a tee time.
