How to Find Cheap Golf Courses

When comparing affordable golf courses, look beyond the regular eighteen-hole price. Different tee times, walking options, and shorter rounds can affect how much a golf outing costs.

  • Play nine holes instead of eighteen when possible.
  • Walk the course instead of renting a cart.
  • Compare weekday and off-peak tee times.
  • Look for student rates and golf course discounts.
  • Borrow or purchase used golf clubs.
  • Practice putting and chipping before paying for more range sessions.

Golf Course Discounts and Student Savings

Some types of golf facilities may offer discounted tee times, twilight rates, or student pricing. Before booking a round, compare the available options and check the facility's current policies.

Students looking for cheap golf courses should also consider how frequently they plan to play. Someone who plays every week may benefit from comparing seasonal passes with individual rounds, while a beginner might spend less by practicing first.
